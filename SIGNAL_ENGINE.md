Index same historical calendar/close and selected latest completed session; user confirms session and unit, local 15:15 cutoff is not exchange/provider guarantee. No new gaps/zero bars accepted. Five outside KT18 checked for strict daily data but remain outside ranking until approved data review. Rank only new -1 to +1 on valid symbols, descending RS60 then Buy Score then ticker; negative RS permitted, A0 unchanged. Other states are virtual strategy states, not account holdings or sells. 120 scan ALL or 4 batches, same index digest and target; results session-local and not durable, timestamped; no account, quantities, orders, legal clearance or forward-validation evidence. Older reference price revisions may block many stocks (intentional fail closed); no provider redundancy. Legacy scanner and all historical research unchanged.
